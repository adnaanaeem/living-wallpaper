Best results: a wide landscape/scenery shot with open sky at the top, taken in flat/overcast light (not a photo with a strong visible sun or hard shadows already in it — the app adds its own sun glow and can clash with one that's already there). Skip photos with people or pets; the relight is a static colour grade plus rain/snow drawn on top, which looks odd on a face. Avoid pre-filtered photos too, since the day/night grade layers on top of whatever colour balance is already there.
